## The thing itself

Caves of Qud is a game about walking out into a ruined, teeming world and coming back
changed. This mod asks a smaller question underneath that one: **what if somewhere out there
were yours?**

You find a shallow tin basin, fill it with fresh water, set it down on ground you mean to
keep, and pour. Whoever is standing there drinks with you. That is the founding — no charter,
no deed, no law, because there is no law out here. Water was shared on that ground in front
of witnesses, and now the place exists.

Then you leave, because you are still an adventurer and the world is still out there. And the
settlement goes on without you: settlers arrive because word travelled, the stores rise and
fall, someone raises a bunk, raiders take an interest, and somebody starts writing it all
down. When you come back, it tells you what happened while you were gone.

## What it is not

**It is not a second job.** The settlement never nags or creates an unseen loss. Switching a
module off banks neither deadlines nor consequences, and resume starts future work from now; an
already-committed recovery remains durable rather than being rolled back or duplicated. Absence
can resolve a previously disclosed brink or let a hand-supported city subside toward what its
works honestly carry; both are bounded, dated, and announced. Optional bonus interactions reward
engagement and do not tax abstention. A player who never founds anything should encounter one
rumour and one ruin, and otherwise never notice this mod exists.

**It is not a management screen.** You set intent — where the ground is claimed, what the
policy is, what gets commissioned — and the settlement acts on it. You will never schedule a
citizen's day. You say how many hands carry water and what gets built; the settlement decides
who. You are the founder, not the foreman.

**It is not a numbers panel.** The treasury is casks you can walk up to. The population is
people with names who came from somewhere. The history is a book that can be read, argued
with, and got wrong.

These refusals define the product. Sim Settlements supplies one useful, narrower comparator
lesson: typed plots, authored plans, and delegated growth can make the player a leader rather than
a foreman. It does not prove a popularity superlative, and it does not license importing Fallout's
virtual economy, opaque autonomy, or household micromanagement. What fills the refusals is below.

## What you do

Found. Claim ground. Reserve a typed lot, then choose the authored building plan that will live
and evolve on it. Set the water detail — how many hands carry, never whose. Set policy for the
gate and the stores. Answer petitions. Answer raids — pay, fight, fortify, or talk. Strike trade
charters. Read the book, and argue with it. And when the place outgrows your own two hands, invest
until it no longer needs them: that ladder — your hands, then other hands, then bigger vessels,
then carts, then pipes — is the spine of progression here.
Automation is bought, not assumed; its absence limits a settlement, and never cripples one.
And that shape is the rule for everything the settlement will ever learn to do — water, word,
warding, healing, anything: first an effortful, primitive rung; then settlers' hands; then
great technology recovered and made trustworthy; and at the top, sometimes, a covenant —
power at a price the system will not judge. Nothing skips rungs, and nothing at the top is
free.

A big city gives much and drinks deep. What feeds it is your choice: haul your adventuring
fortune home, or build the settlement until it carries its own. Both are honest; the second
must be earned. Water, food, materials, bits, and exotic inputs are available only where their
physical custody and lawful route say they are. A full store is capacity, not a magical citywide
aura; a staffed producer, an explicit draw, or a completed transaction is what turns availability
into work or a bounded bonus.

## The pillars

**Any covenant, honestly priced.** The default story is sanctuary — Abram sowing watervine and a
young people finding a home. Resheph's harborage contributes poetic and cosmopolitan resonance as
a cosmic port, not proof of sanctuary, and the histories of Abram and Resheph remain contested.
Qud's nature is that any allegiance is playable, and the system does not judge. The *world* does,
through factions that already have opinions. Where a history is deliberately authored as disputed,
the Chronicle can preserve separate official and outsider accounts; ordinary events keep one
owning record and are not mechanically duplicated.

`Creed` is the save-compatible umbrella for that identity, not a claim that every faction is a
religion. Authored semantic kinds distinguish communities, peoples, polities, orders, doctrines,
and cults. Only doctrines, cults, and explicitly theological orders can participate in belief,
conversion, and shrine consecration. Every other kind uses covenant, affiliation, adoption, and
allegiance; unknown modded factions stay neutral until their own data opts in. No renewal covenant
is mandatory, and every supported covenant, creed, and founding outcome remains playable within
the same content and consequence laws.

Architecture is not ideology. A city answers its ground, available material, technology, creed,
and builders, so two cities of one realm may look unlike each other without an automatic dissent
meter. Conflict needs a caused creed, relationship, decision, or witnessed incident; different
roofs alone never manufacture a grievance.

**Water is covenant.** It is the currency, the greeting, and the social fabric of the setting,
so it is the spine of the settlement: what founds it, what limits it, what threatens it, and
what everything is ultimately paid in.

**The world keeps time; you are told at the door.** Time away produces news to come home to,
never a debt you did not choose. But it is not a pause. The settlement lives while you are
gone: crops ripen, yards shape stock, scaffolds rise, works wear from being *run*, joinable belief moves
between people who share a roof, dissent gathers, and a city standing above what its works carry
settles back toward them. What none of that is, is a clock running on its own. Every productive or
development rate is time × **labour** × infrastructure, so an unstaffed yard shapes nothing over
a season, a scaffold nobody works on does not rise, and a mill left alone wears not at all —
idleness is what costs nothing, and it always was. Consequences are realised when you are made
aware of them, and awareness is **pushed**: when something irreversible reaches its brink — a
settler with nowhere to live, a creed at the end of its road, a city drawing up its own charter
— word finds you wherever you are, once, dated, and it names what would stop it. From that
moment you have the authored response span for that brink, and if you spend it elsewhere the
thing happens anyway, told to you afterward with the day it happened on. A thousand days away
and ten days away still arrive at the same bounded place, because nothing accrues past a brink;
what you cannot be dealt is a loss nobody ever warned you about. Nothing anywhere is forgiven
on a timer, because nothing needs to be: what bounds
the worst of an absence is the level the works honestly carry, and the floor below that is a
camp. You come home to a report of what happened, not an inspection.

The settlement archive's fixed-rate arrival cadence and its lifecycle receipts are the one v1
arrival authority. The earlier Growth-1B/schema-5 oracle is not deferred runtime scope: it froze a
hostile parser and terminal canonical validator only, and explicitly delivered no transitions,
wire, C#, save root, caller, materialization, tuning, or gameplay. Installing it beside current
cadence would create a parallel authority. That path is rejected unless a later ruling replaces
cadence through an explicit migration and complete evidence owner.

**A warned abstract brink may resolve during absence; an unwitnessed physical loss, casualty, or
conquest may not.**

**Loss writes chronicles, not game-overs.** The settlement is woundable and never deletable.
A loaded, witnessed raid may take water or harm people; a previously disclosed departure brink
may resolve; districts may fall into named ruin states. Absence alone never authors a casualty,
conquest, or physical theft. Every lawful loss leaves a scar in the record and a thing to put
right, and there is always a floor below which the settlement cannot fall.

**Hubris subsides.** A city stands on water, roofs, and the civic works within them. Build past
those works and the excess was scaffolding: while you are away, the settlement moves toward what
its live population infrastructure honestly carries — growing to it, or subsiding to it, person
by person and district into ruin — and never past it in either direction. A camp carries itself;
a great city drinks deep. A hundred days and a thousand days write different chronicles, but both end
at the same honest level. Come home to the report of which way it moved; a slide caught
midway can still be arrested. What you invested in persists. What you propped up subsides.
The standing questions of the mid-game are when to expand, why, and on whose water.

**Buildings are people.** A shop is a settler who took up the trade. A working charging post
is two settlers turning a crank. Nothing in the settlement produces anything without someone
to do it.

A civic market exists only while one accepted, staffed `taf:market` provider stands on the
designated ground and its exact office is held. It first opens at Village with current standing 3
or better, and it may honestly open empty. `ShopTier` records current operational standing and
reach—not ware quality or historical high water—and falls, including to zero, when the service
does. Only ordinary Qud TradeUI exchange creates or removes its physical `_stock`: TAF generates no
wares, consignments, periodic restock, passive output, or remote debit. A sealed
`GenericInventoryRestocker` is only the native empty-trade adapter. Goods sold, bought, stolen,
dropped, carried, container-held, foreign-held, or left on a corpse remain their exact physical
objects; TAF may retire only its own receipt and guards. Completed or dormant legendary traders
remain finite personal native merchants through civic loss or accession, but grant no civic market
authority without the live provider and office. Succession excludes only an open prepared market
handoff endpoint while that exact transfer is unresolved.

**Your decisions walk around.** What you chose should be readable off the settlement itself —
who arrived, what they carry, what they refuse, what got built, how a caravan greets you — not
only off the page that records it. If the only way to know what kind of place this became is
to read about it, the place is not expressing anything.

**Disputed history can have two registers.** Only an event authored as a contested history writes
both the settlement's official account and the version outsiders tell — which may drift,
embellish, or flatly deny. Every other event remains in its one owning Chronicle, ledger, or
report; there is no universal pair generator. History in Qud can be contested text, and yours may
be too. Exile and return are deliberate disputes: their
official and outsider accounts are authored separately, frozen and receipted as one exact pair,
and shown through the TAF Chronicle. Exact before/after register hashes and a domain-separated
fingerprint bind both rendered accounts through retry, interruption, and terminal compaction. They
do not write Sultan/world history or a vanilla Journal accomplishment or mural; the existing
opted-in Chronicle-gossip bridge may still carry the outsider account as a tradable observation.

**Speak Qudish or stay silent.** Names come from the setting's own morphology, dates are given
in mutated-Hebrew months and AR years, and every object gets a description worth reading. Prose
is half the simulation. The founding handbook is Neseva Cask-Hand's Uru Ux 1000 AR copy for the
Open Basin fellowship, with a marginal countervoice: useful local custom and mechanical guidance,
not a universal history or law imposed on Qud.

## The physical product model

**A plot is a reserved evolving lot; a lot carries a plan.** A plot is reserved ground with a
stable typed lot identity, not an abstract building bonus and not the building itself. The four
even-axis reservations are S `6x4`, M `8x6`, L `12x10`, and XL `20x18`. A building's behavior
comes from one authored, inspectable plan with an exact footprint, entrances, fixtures, palette,
and tier map. Growth is not universally additive: an explicit transition declares an in-place
addition, additive expansion, renovation, renovation with expansion, or strike-and-fresh
replacement. Walls, floors, rooms, circulation, and stateless furniture may change when the
authored purpose calls for it;
LotId, paid authority, immutable relics, and occupied or state-bearing objects survive or enter an
explicit receipted handover. A larger reservation may host a fuller later plan. Growing the lot
envelope itself first proves adjacent ground, road ingress, and its exact posed map.

The rite-owned civic heart keeps one LotId from basin through waterstone, moot, and great court,
but it obeys the same transformation law rather than a blanket accretion exception. Its immutable
first basin remains on the recorded rite while the surrounding architecture may be rebuilt. The
hosted arcology is not another XL floor plan: its surface gateway is rooted at that court, while
the actual city-shell occupies one complete parasang's 3x3 local-zone grid on each of three
storeys — 27 ordinary zones under one surface authority. Two fixed authority slots retain at most
the current realm and its one archived exiled
realm, so refounding cannot grow save keys without bound. Hosted lots live in persistent zones
inside that bounded shell, spend no additional surface plot, load no remote zone, and simulate no
unloaded actor. When a later heart rung needs
occupied ground, its ring call may offer a complete plan for only the settlement-raised lots already
marked yielding. The ordinary deterministic layout scorer chooses receiving ground (with lawful
founder override); one original whole lot moves at a time after its labour-only frame completes,
preserving the same LotId, objects, architecture, contents, residents, staffing, work, wear, and
declared networks. It spends no water or material and acts only after founder consent. Stage/tier,
knowledge or technology, physical materials, infrastructure,
capable labour, style, creed, and other authored triggers may open a change. Missing gates refuse
before cost and name what is missing. The ruling record is
[Building catalogue brief](_notes/BUILDING-CATALOGUE-BRIEF.md#plots-are-the-unit-of-building),
and the exact lot/occupancy/transition law is in the
[architecture polish contract](_notes/ARCHITECTURE-POLISH-CONTRACT.md#2-product-model).

**Architecture tells material, technology, and belief truth.** A paid timber wall must not
materialise as worked metal; a low-technology plan must not silently render high craft. Building
styles are tags over distinct authored plans, not recolours standing in for difference. A creed
work enforces whichever builder, creed, knowledge, builder-history, and technology gates its row
actually declares; naming `Creed` supplies the default city-share threshold when `CreedShare` is
unsaid, rather than silently adding every possible gate. Culture, creed, species, skills, and
attributes feed the existing knowledge and capability lanes where appropriate; they do not become
ethnic stat packages or a parallel city simulation. See the
[materials and craft law](_notes/ARCHITECTURE-POLISH-CONTRACT.md#7-materials-and-craft-rungs)
and [creed-gate ruling](_notes/BUILDING-CATALOGUE-BRIEF.md#addendum-16-2026-08-22--styles-and-creed-gated-building-ruled-author-answer-to-q3).
Current presentation is vanilla-art/glyph-first. Original art is permitted when it meets the
[asset provenance, human-revision, fallback, and native-review policy](docs/ASSET_PROVENANCE.md); it is
not required to make the architecture structurally distinct. The current runtime deliberately uses
only verified vanilla bitmap paths and glyph fallbacks, with zero custom runtime bitmap paths.
Ordinary Snapjaw caches use a Woven Basket rather than a Chiliad regional skin; Hindren textile
works use an ordinary Sewing Machine as a treadle stitcher rather than Nacham's unique charged wire
extruder; and TAF faction identity uses its own seed-coloured glyph projection rather than Joppa
terrain art. These category corrections are static evidence, not human approval at native scale. The
digest-bound structural census lives in the
[architecture quality ledger](docs/ARCHITECTURE-QUALITY-AUDIT.md) rather than being hand-maintained
here. Exhaustive static validity does not sign native appearance or usability.

**Assent is a witnessed civic resource, not a faction-wide switch.** The assenting moot appears
only in the currently seated city's derived catalogue when that city holds assent research, the
founder carries Chavvah's rite, and claimed surface ground lies cardinally beside the Moon Stair.
That observation is never permanent knowledge. One exact finished moot records at most six named
resident assents and six named exemptions. Only currently valid exact assenting bodies strengthen
its native ambient ward; every granted exemption spends one voice and its exact body may refuse
the ambient effect. Damage, absence, departure, strike, secession, or lost prerequisites suspend
or weaken the projection without changing NormCore or global faction state.

**Food/water availability is physical; city bonuses are earned.** Fresh water sits in dedicated
vessels and retains its existing physical upkeep and scarcity flow. Cultivation begins with seed
and authored crop rows; harvest creates real items; mills transform exact real inputs; dedicated
larders expose the resulting stock to meals, industry, and trade. There is no abstract foraging,
per-capita ration tick, away-time food bill, hunger catch-up, famine mark, food-caused departure,
passive food-rate minting, or unattended spoilage/decay. A shared meal is an explicit, quoted act: it requires spendable
ingredients and a currently capable physical cooking provider on the designated ground, debits
the exact disclosed ingredients once, then grants only its bounded creed/cohabitation effects.
An empty pantry or missing kitchen withholds the act, reports the missing link, and spends nothing.
Stock by itself grants neither an aura nor a penalty, and meal history never alters population
capacity. Food catalogue/city-book fields remain compatibility metadata only: food never enters
the live supported level, binding cause, or subsidence calculation. Zero food with adequate water
and roofs cannot shrink a settlement, and more food cannot raise its base supported population.
Damage may reduce a larder's work effectiveness until repaired, but even the longest absence
preserves every pantry object's identity and count. Frozen food-loss ordinals/receipts normalize
inert before any callback.
Materials likewise remain physical, proved-custody inputs. Production remains labour ×
infrastructure × availability; knowledge may improve a positive output but never tax an abstaining
city or multiply a draw.

**Logistics uses one routing substrate and real routes.** The v1 logistics substrate has frozen
central plans, nearest-holder proofs, exact multi-zone itineraries, city-local porters, a bounded
intercity water manifest whose three-city menu offers both non-seat destinations, and trade
caravans. Each cargo transaction has one owning receipt; the shared
routing substrate does not become a parallel cargo authority. Stable records answer where cargo
is, whose custody it is in, and when a body should render; actors do not literally walk unloaded
world tiles. Stores remain settlement- and zone-local rather than a pooled realm workshop.
Ordinary construction funding now mints one central construction-input job when exact local stock
is absent: the job freezes nearest lawful water/material custody, itinerary, carrier, landing,
debit, rollback, recovery, and master-pause authority before work can begin. It never licenses a
remote debit or pooled stock. This is implemented at code scope; integrated conservation,
interruption, and native evidence remains an active gate. Current proof status belongs to
[STATUS](docs/STATUS.md), the [brief audit](_notes/BRIEF-IMPLEMENTATION-AUDIT.md), and the
[contract/runtime reconciliation](_notes/CONTRACT-RUNTIME-RECONCILIATION-2026-08-25.md).

## Where it stands

**Implemented at code/content scope.** Founding by rite, including ruin reclamation, plus
covenants with living villages; a seat and up to two non-seat cities under one realm; horizontal and vertical
claims; roads, shafts, districts, reserved typed lots, exact authored building plans and tier
maps, typed materials, power, wear, and repair. Water and food are separate physical flows:

explicit capable-kitchen meals, mills, industry, trade, and cross-zone delivery. Food creates no

The semantic foundation is persisted as `KingdomSystem.PolityLedger`: canonical bounded records,
relations, immutable profiles, routes, fronts, grievances, cohorts, figures, incidents, projection
receipts, options, and compaction evidence under a strict current/prior/future wire boundary. It is
the authority consumed by the bounded adapters above, not permission to infer a native result.
Existing ruins, raids, caravans, or manifests never silently prove their expansions.

`KingdomSystem.PolityTransition` owns the separate crash receipt for exile, return, and refounding.
Its rollback ledger bytes exist only while exact return remains possible; a successful refound
destroys that escrow and retains only bounded legacy facts plus old faction tombstone evidence.
No actor id crosses this seam, and no faction id may be reused by the refounded realm.

**Not the v1 model.** A live actor/object graph, inventory, personal quest or reputation state,
and actor faction membership never cross saves. Bounded faction identity facts needed to tell the
inherited site's history may cross; they do not recreate the former actor or their memberships.
Ideological opposition alone never creates war. TAF does not keep armies or caravans walking
unloaded tiles, resolve conquest/casualties unseen, simulate a Dwarf-Fortress-scale background
world, or pool every settlement's stores. Those shapes are rejected because they violate Qud's
physical authority, bounded attention, causal grievance, and no-unseen-loss laws—not because they
are unfinished shortcuts. Every positive polity direction above is accepted v1 work; each row's
remaining adapter and evidence clauses stay open until they close. The current
code/evidence boundary is in
[TESTING.md](TESTING.md#current-implementation-limits-and-v1-scope-gates).

## For people who want to build on it

The mod is designed to be a platform, not a monolith. Buildings, city styles, trade charters,
raid profiles, research, and the supported behavior lanes are registry- or contract-driven:
third-party content extends the declared vocabulary without patching core, and malformed or
version-drifted declarations refuse under their owner rather than partially applying. Building
blueprints are ordinary Qud objects, so lawful vanilla parts and other mods' objects can supply
their existing function. The separate-mod cold-load/materialisation fixture remains an evidence
gate; data loading and source tests alone do not sign ecosystem compatibility.

The engineering rules are in [STANDARDS.md](STANDARDS.md), the extension contract in
[MODDING.md](MODDING.md), and the supported code surface in [docs/API.md](docs/API.md). Two of
those rules matter enough to state here:

**Nothing you place is ever consumed, moved, or destroyed by this mod without your explicit
say-so.** Storage joins the settlement only when dedicated. Automatic placement targets empty
ground only. This holds for other mods' structures as much as your own dropped waterskin.

**Failures are contained by authority.** Noncritical presentation and optional work may log and
skip under their exact owner. Unsafe mutation, ambiguous custody, malformed current authority, or
an unprovable save transition fails closed and may quarantine that lane or veto load/save; it never
silently resets, guesses, or lets corruption propagate. Most people run this beside many mods, so
containment is strict without pretending every fault is safely ignorable.
