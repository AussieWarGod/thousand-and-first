## Vocabulary

- **SHIP** — part of the current v1 scope and implemented at the stated boundary. Native/human
  evidence may still be open, but no absent speculative system is labeled `SHIP`.
- **AUTHOR-DEFERRED** — deliberately outside v1 until the author explicitly reopens the exact row.
  It must not be implied by UI, Workshop prose, tests, or screenshots.
- **REJECTED** — incompatible with a controlling law. It is not engineering debt and must not be
  smuggled in as an implementation shortcut.
- **IMPLEMENTED / PARTIAL / MISSING / RESEARCH ONLY** — current runtime verdict, independent of
  scope disposition. Portable/source proof never promotes a row past native/human evidence.

## Author-deferred breadth

| Item | Why it is outside v1 |
|---|---|
| Any living successor/namesake/claimant polity surface | The original idea was a question, and final research requires owning adapters plus recognition evidence. Exact same-person continuation remains rejected. |
| One or more substantive legacy rivals, or fresh procedural rival generation | First bounded rival must prove attention, save, conflict, and route costs after explicit reopening. |
| Generalized diplomats/emissaries, guards/patrols, visible intercity/rival traffic, polity clashes, or route correspondence | Existing owning lanes are real substrate; no shared polity/cohort/off-seat authority is approved for v1. |
| A third owned city | Existing two-seat isolation, routing, density, and native performance must close first. |
| Fully hosted zone-spanning arcology ground | Current work is an honest foundation/surface prototype; hosted interior zones need a separate physical-authority design. |
| Strategic army layer or player-directed conquest campaign | A finite witnessed cohort may ship under V7/V9; a strategic layer needs a new author ruling and experience contract. |

## Rejected implementation shapes

- Exact old actor/object graph, inventory, level/mutation ranks, quest, reputation, or faction key
  crossing saves.
- Automatic war from opposing creed, ethics, species, origin, biome, charter, or standing alone.
- Persistent armies, caravans, lords, or traders walking unloaded world tiles.
- Offscreen conquest, casualties, citizen loss, or irreversible third-party result without the
  separately consented/escrowed exception already defined by the threat contract.
- Pooled realm workshops/resources, observation-dependent supply, or one settlement silently
  spending another's stock.
- Mass rival-settlement/world-war simulation, renewable cohort loot/XP farms, or named-cast quotas.

## Closure rule

`SHIP` means only the implemented bounded surface written in its cell; native/human closure still
requires the named current protocol. Existing raids/caravans/inherited ruins do not silently prove
their `AUTHOR-DEFERRED` expansions. Moving deferred work into v1 requires an explicit author ruling,
an owning contract/adapter, tests, a numbered native protocol, and current-build human evidence.
