## Current ruling and withdrawn claims

The latest direct author ruling supersedes every older abstraction that treated a plot and a
building as the same thing:

- A **plot is only a reserved lot**.
- A behavior-bearing **building plan** occupies that lot and grows through authored tier maps.
- Growth may depend on time, materials, labour, skills, technology, knowledge, demand, creed,
  style, and triggers.
- Architecture must be distinct, Qud-literate, and appropriate to the people who build and live
  there.
- Rendered materials must agree with available technology and the material bill. A stone-age
  building may not silently become metal architecture.
- Original, human-grade bitmap art is allowed where vanilla art or a glyph is insufficient.
- The current goal is a first visual, architectural, interaction, and usability polish pass across
  buildings, plots, roads, fields, utilities, vertical works, megastructures, citizens, effects,
  reports, prompts, wishes, preview art, and Workshop presentation.

Until the gates below pass, the following earlier release-candidate interpretations are
**withdrawn**:

1. that the then-counted 106 building records were tested as sensible, usable buildings;
2. that a generic rectangle, one door, a center object, and population-table furniture constitute
   an authored building blueprint or tier map;
3. that catalogue/style/skin coverage proves distinct architecture;
4. that a claimed vertical reach proves a physical shaft the player or a citizen can traverse;
5. that one restored object per old work proves inherited layout or legible streets;
6. that the present theatre, annexe, and arcology representations prove end-state architecture;
7. that low standing alone proves a raid was caused by a specific provocation;
8. that the automated release smoke proves the human visual, usability, pacing, compatibility, or
   lived-city gates.

The current and historical loader/persistence smokes remain valid **only at their recorded
scopes**. `TESTING.md`'s current and historical smoke sections explicitly exclude human,
full-gallery, compatibility, and complete-protocol signoff.

Verdicts below mean:

- **Met:** evidence supports the documented promise at the stated scope.
- **Partial:** substantial implementation exists, but a required part is missing.
- **Proxy:** code records or reports an abstract fact without implementing the promised physical or
  player-facing behavior.
- **Absent:** no runtime route/state implementing the named behavior was found.
- **Unverified:** implementation exists, but the required live/human gate has not run.
- **Active gap:** accepted positive v1 work whose implementation or named evidence is incomplete.
