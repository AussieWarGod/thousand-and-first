# Attended construction-input custody

Remote construction inputs remain exact objects. No economic stock pool or unloaded actor
simulation represents them.

## Partition order

Cancellation has one deterministic next partition: retract each landed child at the attended
target, restore independent sources in ordinal order but later debits of the same frozen source
first, retire each empty child
at its attended source, then close central rows. Visiting any other touched zone is a no-write
wait. A former claim may run only this receipt-owned lane (or a debit/commit that already won),
after external-ownership checks; it runs no city economy. A durable round-robin cursor bounds a
pass to eight attempted mutations without letting stale earlier receipts starve later custody.

## Custody proof

Every callback proves the parent receipt, unique child row, manifest schema/digest/revision,
binding object ID, exact direct parent, reference count, marker type/value, amount, and a bounded
whole `GetContents` graph. Missing, stale, corrupt, ambiguous, extra, aliased, or foreign custody
refuses without seizure or quarantine. Semantic-root bodies are read-only until the exact body is
materialized on the attended source.

Creation and split markers are durable write-ahead identities. Cancellation uniquely adopts a
marked cask or split remainder from its exact carrier/holder before cleanup. Water source `Before`
is reproved before cask creation or property mutation.

## Retirement and save cuts

Carrier retirement publishes `intent:<TripId>:<ObjectId>` before `Obliterate`, requires exact
empty porter graveyard evidence, publishes `retired:<TripId>:<ObjectId>`, then unbinds. Central rows close only after every child
retirement witness, binding, transit root, body, and whole-custody postcondition is proved. The
retirement witness remains until the parent receipt becomes terminal; global terminal recovery
clears a witness left by a save cut.

Schema 3 is current. Schema-2 receipts remain canonical. An old unrooted `InFlight` body is adopted
only when its exact binding and complete manifest are visible at its attended frozen source; its
semantic root is published before context removal. No cached or offscreen zone is loaded.

Exile/refounding is fail-closed while a nonterminal or quarantined parent, construction child row,
transient binding, transit root, or retirement witness remains. These value-bearing global states
are preservation-classified for removal; absent-mod cleanup is not promised.
