## Preserved hard boundaries

Un-deferral does not revive rejected machinery:

- no exact old actor/object continuation across legacy boundaries;
- no actors walking unloaded tiles, remote generation, or zone thaw for story progress;
- no unseen death, casualty, conquest, wounds, theft, or other invisible loss;
- no persistent strategic armies, general war simulation, or ideology/standing-only war;
- no borrowed world actors, actor seizure, allegiance overwrite, or original-artifact custody;
- no pooled cross-city/realm stock, remote direct debit, or shared cargo mutation authority;
- no expiring civic cards, visit timers, catch-up backlogs, passive-yield chores, or hidden penalties;
- no mood/favorite score, story director, bond graph, or shadow constitution; and
- no claim that an absent mod can rewrite or clean a save after its code no longer runs.
